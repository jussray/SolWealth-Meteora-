# Solwealth Birth Contract

Solwealth is a child system created from proven primitives, not a runtime composition of its parents.

## What "born from" means

The birth process takes architectural DNA that already survived implementation and verification, then re-expresses it natively inside a new independent system.

### SleepWealth DNA

Inherited:

- separate capability from authority
- observe before proposing
- require explicit human approval before simulated effect-bearing experience
- preserve outcome evidence
- learn from a sandbox before wider scopes are even considered

Not inherited:

- SleepWealth product identity
- SleepWealth UI
- its broker factory
- its deployment topology
- any live-provider credential or account assumption

### SolContinuity DNA

Inherited:

- multiple observations before calling a state verified
- disagreement is evidence, not something to hide
- fail closed on insufficient evidence
- portable continuity receipts

Not inherited:

- SolContinuity deployment or dashboard
- its provider registry
- its runtime as a required dependency

### FCR / Chief DNA

Inherited:

- truth and evidence remain distinct from authority
- no self-certification
- bounded actions
- a decision cannot magically convert itself into permission

## Independence invariant

Solwealth must boot, observe, propose, simulate, reflect, remember, and verify its own memory with every parent repository offline.

## Development invariant

Experience can recommend developmental progression. It cannot mint authority. A human approval is required to change developmental stage, and even graduation does not change the financial authority ceiling.
